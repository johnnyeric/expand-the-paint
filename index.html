Expand the Paint

You are a little sprayer. Paint is how you grow and how you fight — same tank, same stream, no size buff.

  • Claim the floor. Last sprayer standing wins, or most coverage if the timer ends.
  • On your colour: move faster and refill. Off it: the tank drains.
  • Opposing streams collide, eat paint, and shove you back. The border moves between you.
  • Lose the floor (coverage at zero) and you're out. An empty tank is not the end — paint a path home and refill.

On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and sprays. Host a match to play together on another device.
